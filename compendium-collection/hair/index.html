    hair #

    youtube channels #

    all these youtube channels were great resources when i was taking care of my hair and growing it out. all of them struggled with hari loss or thinning to varying degrees.

    the main takeaways:

    • https://www.youtube.com/@heatherkarra

      • best hair results. she has insanely thick and long hair; struggled with hair loss
      • closest hair type to my own
      • scalp massage and red light is really important
      • stress relief and an overall orientation towards self care is the most important
      • a well balanced diet is important for hair growth

    • https://www.youtube.com/@selfmaxxer

      • sunlight is important
      • scalp massage is important; we need to massage out scalp tension

    • https://www.youtube.com/@invertage

      • inversion introduces blood flow to the scalp and can reverse hair loss
      • he recovered his hair from almost completely bald!

    care and growth #

    health #

    • getting enough protein
    • getting enough vitamins (e.g. through fruit and vegetable intake, supplements if dietary changes aren’t enough)
    • if you lose a lot of body weight, your body will start shedding hair until your weight stabilizes
    • get sunlight on your skin and scalp (vitamin d, red and near infrared light)

    mechanical factors #

    • satin (or rather, commonly satin-like polyester) bonnet and/or pillowcase for sleeping
    • wearing protective hairstyles msot of the time, for long hair
    • don’t rip through tangles. ripping your hair makes it shorter, duh
    • i use a silicone scalp brush when shampooing.
    • use scalp massage with fingers or hair brush. keep the fingers in one place and move the scalp around over the skull

    hair care #

    • i don’t use sodium lauryl sulfate shampoos
    • i apply hair oil to my ends and scalp before washing (rosemary oil, castor/jajoba/avocado oil)
    • i like shea moisture shampoo/conditioner, or VIORI shampoo/conditioner bars, or dr. groot shampoo!
