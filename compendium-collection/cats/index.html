    litter #

    • wood pine pellets are amazing. they are low scent, urine turns into sawdust, feces are easy to scoop out, and most importantly, there aren’t little pebbles that scatter throughout your entire house.
    • there’s also not irritating dust that gets in your and your cat’s lungs.
    • Tractor Supply Co. Horse Bedding Pine Pellets are the most convenient and cheapest option I found.
      • I didn’t want to use fuel pellets because there was a risk of harmful added chemicals.

    harnesses #

    • walking your cat is awesome.
    • Mynwood Cat Jackets are awesome. I got Muffin custom-made harnesses because they were pretty much the same price as the pre-made ones.
      • they’re made by this british lady
      • they have the best design of any cat harness and are escape-resistant. buckle-style harnesses and harnesses for dogs are often too rigid and cats can easily escape from them.
      • they’re customizable (fabric, embroidery, reflective strips, airtag pockets, tripod/three-legged cats etc.)
